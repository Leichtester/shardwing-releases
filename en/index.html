Open your .miz and turn it into a briefing package like a real squadron would – cartridges, lineup cards, kneeboards and timing, planned together and written back to DCS without losing a thing.

Cartridges (DTC)

Steerpoints, threats, geo lines, radios and CMDS for the F-16C and F/A-18C – as a native .dtc that loads in the jet just like one from the Mission Editor.

DTC editor

Lineup Card & Kneeboards

Lineup card, comm card, route map, attack card, SPINS, abnormals – embedded in the mission as kneeboard pages, in German or English, or as a PDF.

Briefing material (German)

Timing

H-hour, TOT and vul windows, push points, tanker slots. The sync matrix shows who is early or late according to their route.

Sync matrix (German)

Intel without a god view

Each flight only sees the threats released to it – on the map, in the DTC, on the kneeboard and in F10.

Releasing intel (German)

Plan together

The mission designer shares the mission, pilots grab a slot via a link and enter their own data – live for everyone.

Sharing a mission (German)

Lossless

Shardwing only changes what you edit. The rest of the mission stays byte for byte as the Mission Editor wrote it.
