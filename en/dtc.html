DTC – Cartridges ​

Since DCS introduced the Data Transfer Cartridge, the jet loads steerpoints, radios and countermeasures from a file. Shardwing builds these cartridges for the F-16C and F/A-18C – in the native DCS format, as if they came from the Mission Editor's DTC manager.

Creating a cartridge ​

Tab DTC of a flight:

ButtonWhat happens
Neu aus Route (new from route)Takes over the waypoints as steerpoints, the radio presets and only the threats released to this flight. CMDS stays with the pilot by default. The usual choice.
Neu leer (new, empty)An empty cartridge – with radios, CMDS and switches from your default cartridge, if you have set one.
Aus Datei … (from file)Load an existing .dtc, e.g. your own from Saved Games\DCS\DTC.

A cartridge in the mission is a file inside the .miz. It is attached to one or more aircraft; the editor's header shows which ones (an Aerial-1, i.e. "on Aerial-1"). Several flights can share one cartridge.

The editor's header ​

  • Name – becomes the file name inside the .miz.
  • an … (on …) – which units of the flight load the cartridge.
  • AutoLoad – loads automatically on spawn. Without AutoLoad, the pilot loads it on the DTE page (F-16) or MUMI page (F/A-18).
  • Als meine Standard-Cartridge (as my default cartridge) – the radios, CMDS and switches of this cartridge become the template for Neu leer (without waypoints, lines, threats).
  • .dtc exportieren (export .dtc) – save as a single file, e.g. for Saved Games\DCS\DTC.
  • Entfernen (remove) – take the cartridge out of the mission.

Sections and "overwrite" ​

A cartridge consists of sections (NAV, COMM, CMDS …). Each section has the switch überschreiben (overwrite):

  • on – written into the jet when loading.
  • off – left out of the .dtc; the jet keeps what it has.

This way you can set steerpoints and radios but leave the pilot's CMDS programs untouched.

Übernehmen aus … (take from …) copies a section from another cartridge – your own CMDS programs, for example.

Editing steerpoints ​

  • Click on the map to place a new steerpoint, drag to move it. When the list is full (e.g. 24 NAV points), placing on the map stops by itself.
  • In the table: type, note, coordinates, Elev ft, TOS, OA1/OA2 and the routes (1, 2, 3 – as in the DTC manager).
  • Elev = Boden (alle) (elevation = ground, all) sets the elevation of every point from terrain data (SRTM, may differ from DCS by a few metres).
  • TOS verschieben (shift TOS) by ±1 or ±5 minutes; TOS aus Route (TOS from route) takes over the ETAs of the mission route. TOS is stored in Zulu; ↑/↓ in the time field changes the digit under the cursor, with Shift in steps of ten.
  • ⌖ auf … (onto …) below the coordinates puts the steerpoint on a place the designer has set: your target times with a location, the tanker track and, under Weitere Planungspunkte (more planning points), every planning point of your side (fix, RV, CP …), even without a time for your flight. That way you place shared waypoints without typing coordinates.
  • Arrows ↑ ↓ change the order, × deletes.

Times: flight plan or on their own ​

The steerpoint times (TOS, ETA on the F/A-18C) come from the flight plan on the Route tab. A new cartridge is coupled to it: the time column here only shows them, and on saving or exporting the .dtc the plan's times are written into the cartridge.

The switch Zeiten getrennt planen (plan times separately) cuts that link: you type the times here and the flight plan leaves them alone. Cartridges from a file or from the mission start out separate; An den Flugplan koppeln on the Route tab couples them.

Geo lines from drawings ​

Aus Grafik übernehmen … / Aus Zeichnung übernehmen (take from graphic / drawing) turns a FLOT, kill box or CAP station into a geo line – into the next free line or appended to a specific one. Areas are closed, racetracks are stepped.

COMM ​

Any station on any channel, in the band of your choice. The F-16 only takes UHF on COMM1 and VHF/FM on COMM2; the Hornet takes both. Easier than channel by channel: set up the package-wide comm plan under Kneeboards → Comm Plan and apply it to all cartridges with one button.

Personal cartridges ​

When planning together, several aircraft often share a cartridge. If a pilot changes their CMDS or colours, Shardwing creates a personal copy (<cartridge> - <callsign>). Their jet loads the copy, everyone else keeps the shared one. A cartridge loaded by several flights can only be changed by the designer – a flight can make its own copy.

Single cartridges ​

If you open a .dtc file without a mission, it appears on the left under Einzelne Cartridges (single cartridges). You can edit it and save it as a .dtc again – your personal default cartridge, for example.
